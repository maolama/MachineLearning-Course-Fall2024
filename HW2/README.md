# HW2 — Classification: Binary, Multi-class and Multi-label

**Goal:** Build classifiers and learn to evaluate them properly.

- **Q1 – Binary classification (moons dataset):** KNN, ROC/AUC, precision–recall curves, and choosing the decision threshold that maximizes F1.
- **Q2 – Multi-class (mobile device usage):** cleaning, EDA and comparing several classifiers to predict one of five user-behavior classes, with feature-importance analysis.
- **Q5 – Multi-label (Yeast dataset):** One-vs-Rest compared with Classifier Chains, plus an ensemble of chains with random label orders.
